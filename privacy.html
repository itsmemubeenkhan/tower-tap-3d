Privacy Policy for Tower Tap 3D

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Tower Tap 3D is developed and published by TheBrandMaker (support@thebrandmaker.com). This page explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

Tower Tap 3D does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Gameplay data such as your best score and settings (sound, music) is stored only on your own device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" in-app purchase (one-time or subscription) is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details. Google's handling of purchase information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

The app does not integrate advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or social SDKs.

Children

Tower Tap 3D does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data deletion

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to delete. You can erase all on-device game data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@thebrandmaker.com.
